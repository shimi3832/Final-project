📌 About This System

Tindahan ni Mare is a digital management system designed for sari-sari stores. It simplifies tracking of inventory, sales, credit (utang), purchases, and store reports — all in one easy-to-use platform with role-based access for Admin, Owner, and Employee.

✨ Key Features

  • Inventory & Product Management
  • Sales Recording with Receipt Printing
  • Utang (Credit) Tracking with Due Dates
  • Supplier & Purchase / Restock Management
  • Sales Reports & Charts with Export
  • Role-Based Access Control (Admin / Owner / Employee)
  • Email Notifications & Recovery via Gmail
  • Cloud Sync via Firebase Firestore
  • Account Recovery
  • Dark Mode Support
